# YouGlass 2.2.1

This hotfix improves the main video player's controls.

- Playback buttons sit along the bottom of the video, with captions positioned above the controls.
- Hover tracking follows the video area and gives you a short grace period when moving away, reducing flicker while reaching for a button.
- Player bounds keep the loading image from displacing playback controls.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Choose **YouGlass > Check for Updates…** to update through Sparkle. Your existing accounts, settings, Library, and playback history remain in place.
