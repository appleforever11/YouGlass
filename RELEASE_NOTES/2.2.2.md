# YouGlass 2.2.2

More reliable hover controls in the main video player.

- Playback controls recover when a pointer entry event is missed, including after scrolling, resizing, or returning to the app.
- Hover follows the actual video bounds, preventing stale pointer events from leaving the controls in the wrong state.
- Opening the player preserves controls that have already appeared under your pointer.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Choose **YouGlass > Check for Updates…** to update through Sparkle. Existing accounts, settings, Library, and playback history remain in place.
