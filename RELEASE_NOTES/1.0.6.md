# YouGlass 1.0.6

This release gives YouGlass a brighter, more expressive Liquid Glass visual system while keeping playback and account behavior stable.

## Highlights

- Added a living pink-and-purple ambience that gently breathes throughout the home and player surfaces.
- Updated dark and light palettes with richer magenta, violet, and blue-violet accents while retaining a black-focused dark mode.
- Refined translucent surfaces, borders, sidebar material, toolbar hierarchy, and section accents for a more coherent Liquid Glass presentation.
- Added subtle pointer lift and pink/purple hover feedback to video cards without changing their layout.
- Added a Reduce ambient motion setting and respected macOS Reduce Motion accessibility preferences.
- Preserved existing YouTube sign-in, personalized feeds, subscriptions, comments, captions, playback checkpoints, PIP, and Sparkle preferences.

## Compatibility

- Apple Silicon only.
- macOS 14 or later.
- No new account or YouTube API permissions are required.
