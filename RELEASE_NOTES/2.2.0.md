# YouGlass 2.2.0

Organize your subscriptions by topic and get recommendations closer to YouTube Home.

## New

- Subscription Groups: create named sidebar folders, add channels through the editor, right-click menu, or drag and drop, and choose alphabetical or manual channel order. Collapse groups or open one to see its channels' latest uploads.
- Home now uses the actual recommendations returned by YouTube for the app's web session, preserving their order. Open **YouTube Home** from the dashboard to view or sign into that session. Recommendations may differ from a separate Safari or Chrome session.
- Cached Home recommendations retain their order when returning from other destinations. The existing account-based recommendation mix remains available when YouTube Home cannot be loaded.

## Fixed

- PiP playback controls stay correctly sized and inside the window as you resize it.
- Enlarging PiP no longer pushes the video outside the visible area and leaves a black picture.
- Preserved the normal player layout and prevented canceled subscription loads from replacing the next page's status with an error.

## Upgrade

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Choose **YouGlass > Check for Updates…** to update through Sparkle. Existing credentials, sessions, preferences, Library data, and playback history remain in place. Subscription Groups organize channels locally without changing your YouTube subscriptions. Shorts remain excluded.
